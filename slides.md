# The $100B+ Pain in Specialty Crops

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-6 mt-6">
  <div class="p-5 bg-slate-800/80 rounded-xl border border-slate-700 text-left">
    <h3 class="text-lg font-bold text-red-400 mb-2">Eyeball Sampling in $1,600/Acre Orchards</h3>
    <ul class="text-xs text-slate-300 space-y-2">
      <li>• <b>50% of Ag Value on 7% of Land</b>: Specialty crops (apples, viticulture, nuts) have massive input costs but zero digital inventory.</li>
      <li>• <b>1-2% Eyeball Sampling</b>: Farm managers manually count a fraction of 1% of trees and extrapolate across entire valleys.</li>
      <li>• <b>Extreme Climate Volatility</b>: Late frosts (€3.3B EU single-night loss) & heatwaves render historical yield tables useless.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="p-5 bg-slate-800/80 rounded-xl border border-slate-700 text-left">
    <h3 class="text-lg font-bold text-amber-400 mb-2">The Financial Cascade</h3>
    <div class="text-xs text-slate-300 space-y-3">
      <div class="p-2 bg-slate-900 rounded border border-slate-800"><b>70% Chemical Waste</b>: Broadcast spraying without canopy-level prescription maps.</div>
      <div class="p-2 bg-slate-900 rounded border border-slate-800"><b>~$1M Co-Op Penalties</b>: 10% yield forecasting error forces ~$1M in post-harvest logistics mismatches per cooperative.</div>
    </div>
