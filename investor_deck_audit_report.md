## 🏗️ SECTION 3: DEEP-DIVE EVALUATION ACROSS 6 VC CORE DIMENSIONS

### 1. Narrative Arc & Slide Flow (Score: 9.0/10)
- **Structure**: The deck follows the classic Sand Hill Road storyline: Pain ($1.5T Blind Spot) → Failed Legacy Alternatives → Decoupled Aerial Solution → Grounded Physics Moat → Competitive Domination → Bottom-Up Math → Pure SaaS Economics → Execution Speed → Scalable Channel GTM → World-Class Team → Legal Hygiene → The Ask.
- **Upgrade**: Ensure the transition between Slide 3 (Decoupled Engine) and Slide 4 (Deterministic Moat) explicitly emphasizes: *"We don't buy drones, we ingest photon data."*

### 2. Market Math & Unit Economics (Score: 7.5/10)
- **The Core Issue**: You currently model SOM at $150M based on 25M acres at $6/acre. But your core monetization driver—automated CV blossom/fruitlet load and 3D canopy analysis—commands $20–$100/acre (Advanced Tier) and $200+/acre (Enterprise Tier).
- **The Russ Correction**: 
  - *Baseline Entry*: $6/acre/year (Thermal CWSI & Terrain)
  - *Core Expansion*: $35/acre average blended yield (CV Fruitlet Thinning + 3D PAR)
  - *Re-calculated SOM*: 25M Acres × $35/acre = **$875M SOM**
  - *Re-calculated SAM*: 350M Acres × $35/acre = **$12.25B SAM**
  - Frame the $6 rate as the low-friction land-and-expand wedge, and $35+ as the ACV expansion.

### 3. Technical Moat & Defensive Science (Score: 9.5/10)
- **Defensibility**: High-resolution multispectral and thermal radiometric calibration combined with deterministic crop physics (CWSI equations + 3D PAR light interception) creates a defensible data moat.
- **VC Narrative**: Positioning against "Black-Box AI Wrappers" is brilliant in the current 2026 market climate where VCs are exhausted by hallucinating LLM wrappers. Grounded physics = defensible IP.

### 4. Go-To-Market & Channel Scalability (Score: 9.0/10)
- **Flywheel**: Partnering with commercial drone service providers and AgTech resellers who already have boots on the ground decouples customer acquisition CAC from physical flight logistics.
- **Economics**: Resellers charge growers for flight operations; Arbor ingests raw flight imagery via cloud API and delivers spatial analytics at **80%+ software gross margin**.

### 5. Founder-Market Fit & Team Credibility (Score: 9.5/10)
- **Triumvirate Strength**:
  - **Michael Chang (CEO)**: Deep technical domain authority (IEEE spectral publishing, commercial drone pilot) leading US commercial expansion.
  - **Edwin Pircher (COO)**: Unfair unfair regional GTM advantage (South Tyrol apple farming dynasty background + international automation sales).
  - **Simon Lasselsberger (CTO)**: Proven track record of high-scale software engineering (**Scaled Runtastic → $240M Adidas Exit**).

### 6. Deal Structure & SAFE Terms (Score: 8.0/10)
- **Legal Setup**: Single Delaware C-Corp parent with 100% consolidated IP, controlling an EU R&D subsidiary. 80% founder equity retaining control post-accelerator.
- **Improvement Needed**: Specify the Valuation Cap ($8M–$10M post-money cap) on the $500k SAFE ask.
